FAQ

Frequently asked questions about medical & surgical supplies in Sri Lanka

Where can I buy medical and surgical supplies in Sri Lanka?

Hettiarachchi Surgicals (surgicals.lk) is an NMRA-registered medical equipment and surgical consumables supplier with a showroom in Galle and island-wide delivery. Browse our catalog of wheelchairs, hospital beds, diagnostics, surgical instruments and home-care equipment, then order via WhatsApp for a same-day quotation — from anywhere in Sri Lanka, including Colombo.

Do you supply hospitals and clinics, or only individuals?

Both. We supply hospitals, clinics and private practitioners with multi-item quotes and stamped quotation PDFs (BR/VAT/SVAT handled for tenders), as well as home-care families and retail/first-aid buyers ordering single items via WhatsApp.

What medical equipment and consumables do you stock?

Our catalog covers mobility aids (wheelchairs, commodes, canes, crutches, walking frames), hospital furniture (beds, examination tables, patient trolleys), diagnostics (BP monitors, glucometers, pulse oximeters, stethoscopes, thermometers), supports & braces (cervical collars, lumbar supports, arm slings), surgical instruments and staplers, anti-decubitus mattresses, and home-care essentials like adult diapers and suction units.

Are your medical products NMRA registered?

Yes. Hettiarachchi Surgicals sources from an NMRA-compliant catalog, making our equipment suitable for hospitals, clinics and home use across Sri Lanka.

How do I get a price quote for medical supplies?

Send us a WhatsApp message (Galle: 071 820 8654, Colombo: 071 966 9666) with the items, quantities and your delivery location, or use the quote form on this page. Our team replies the same business day with a stamped quotation.

Do you deliver medical equipment to Colombo and other cities?

Yes. While our showroom is in Galle, we deliver island-wide and take orders from Colombo and across Sri Lanka via WhatsApp — no showroom visit required.
