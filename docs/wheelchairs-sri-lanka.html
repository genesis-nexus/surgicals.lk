Buying guide

Choosing the right wheelchair

Adult wheelchairs are a good fit for general home or hospital mobility — lightweight, foldable and easy to transport in a car.

Functional wheelchairs add adjustable footrests for users who need extra comfort during longer periods of sitting.

Fully-functional wheelchairs include elevating leg rests, useful for post-operative recovery, swelling management or limited leg mobility.

Commode wheelchairs combine a mobility chair with a toileting function — a practical choice for home care where bathroom access is difficult.

Not sure which one fits your situation? Send us a WhatsApp message describing the user's needs (home, hospital, post-surgery, etc.) and our team will recommend the right model.
