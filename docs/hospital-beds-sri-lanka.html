Buying guide

How to choose a hospital bed

Two-function beds adjust the back and knee sections — the standard choice for general wards and home nursing. Head-adjustable beds are the simplest, most affordable option for home use.

Three-function beds add height adjustment. Our Three Function Bed — Manual (MG1016) is Rs. 182,000, with a crank-operated frame, collapsible side rails and an adjustable height of 430–700 mm.

Five-function beds add Trendelenburg positioning and a hydraulic lift for ICU, high-dependency and post-operative care; the hydraulic models include an IV pole.

Electric beds (MG1022) give one-touch powered backrest and knee adjustment with the mattress included — the best choice for long-term home patients and caregivers.

Pair any bed with an anti-decubitus air mattress to help prevent pressure sores. Not sure which model fits? Send the patient's needs on WhatsApp and we will recommend the right bed and mattress in one quotation.

What is the difference between a manual, hydraulic and electric hospital bed?

Manual beds use a hand crank to raise the back and knee sections and are the most economical. Hydraulic beds add a hydraulic lift for height and Trendelenburg positioning. Electric beds use motors for one-touch adjustment of the backrest and knee sections, which makes caregiving easier for long-term and home patients.

What do two-, three- and five-function hospital beds mean?

A two-function bed adjusts the back and knee sections. A three-function bed adds height adjustment. A five-function bed adds Trendelenburg and reverse-Trendelenburg tilt for ICU and post-operative use. Every function can be manual, hydraulic or electric depending on the model.
