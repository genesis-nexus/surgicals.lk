Buying guide

Choosing the right hospital bed or furniture

Two-function beds adjust the back and knee sections manually — the standard choice for general wards and home nursing where powered adjustment isn't required.

Three- and five-function beds add height adjustment and (on five-function models) Trendelenburg positioning, in manual crank, hydraulic or electric versions — specify electric where caregivers need one-touch repositioning.

Examination and gynecological beds come in epoxy powder coated or stainless steel. Stainless steel suits high-traffic clinical areas that are disinfected frequently; epoxy steel offers the same geometry at a lower price point.

Patient transport, transfer and recovery trolleys differ by top surface: transfer models have removable tops for moving patients between trolley and bed, while recovery trolleys add height adjustment and backrests for post-operative observation.

Not sure which configuration fits your ward or home setup? Send us the intended use on WhatsApp and our team will recommend the right model, mattress and accessories in one quotation.
